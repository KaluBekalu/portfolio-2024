## Overview

[Guaro](https://guaro-web.fly.dev) is a cloud-hosted inventory, point-of-sale, and buying-trip manager built for small neighborhood shops. It covers the full retail loop: track stock, ring up sales, plan restocking trips with multi-currency costs, and watch the business from a dashboard — with multi-shop accounts and per-shop roles.

## Key Features

- **True multi-tenancy**: One account can belong to multiple shops with owner/staff roles per shop — and tenant isolation is enforced at the database level with Postgres Row-Level Security, not just application code.
- **Buying-trip logistics**: Records purchases in foreign currency with FX-rate snapshots, tracks overhead separately, and computes per-trip profit & loss.
- **Inventory depth**: SKU/barcode tracking, product variants, base-unit vs pack-unit pricing, low-stock and expiry alerts, and a full adjustment history.
- **POS & dashboard**: Fast sales ringup and a business dashboard for the day-to-day pulse of the shop.

## Tech Stack

A pnpm monorepo with two apps and three shared packages: a Next.js 15 (App Router) frontend with Tailwind CSS 4, and a Fastify 5 API with Zod validation and a generated [Swagger/OpenAPI surface](https://guaro-api.fly.dev/docs). Drizzle ORM manages Neon Postgres, with Biome, Vitest, and Husky keeping quality gates automatic.

## CI/CD

GitHub Actions runs lint, typecheck, build, and tests on every pull request; merging to `main` deploys both apps to Fly.io with automatic database migrations — zero-touch from merge to production.
